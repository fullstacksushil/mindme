# Mind-Me help
> A map of how Mind-Me works, made in Mind-Me. Open a branch to read about it. Try things freely: Help starts fresh each time you open it.
- Getting started
  > Every map grows from one idea. Type it on the start screen and choose how to grow it.
  - Brainstorm
    > Enter starts a map of your idea. The AI suggests a few branches at a time, and you keep the ones you like.
  - Map out
    > Ctrl+Enter, or Map out, asks the AI for a whole map of a topic at once, three levels deep, with a note on every node.
  - Grill me
    > Tick Grill me first, and the AI asks you a few questions about your goal and level before it starts, so the map fits you.
  - Templates
    > A template starts the map with fixed branches, such as Pros and cons or SWOT, and your idea at the root.
- Suggestions
  > The AI's ideas show as ghosts: soft, italic boxes around the current node. Nothing is saved until you keep one.
  - Keep a suggestion
    > Click a ghost, or select it with the arrow keys and press Enter. It becomes a real branch.
  - More ideas
    > M, or More ideas, replaces the ghosts on show with new ones. It asks first, because the old ones go.
  - Auto-suggest
    > With Auto-suggest on, opening a node asks the AI for ghosts. Turn it off to explore a map without asking the AI.
- Growing by hand
  > You can build a whole map without the AI.
  - Add a branch
    > Tab opens a box for a new branch under the current node. Enter adds it and moves to it, so you can build a chain.
  - Rename
    > Double-click a node, or edit the name at the top of the topic panel.
  - Move
    > Drag a node onto another to move it there with all its branches. In the outline, drag between two entries to reorder. Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V move from the keyboard.
  - Delete
    > Delete removes a node and its branches, after a yes. Only the root stays.
  - Undo
    > Ctrl+Z undoes any change to the tree, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes it.
- Notes and sources
  > Every node has a note in the topic panel, written in Markdown, with LaTeX math between dollar signs.
  - Learn more
    > L, or Learn more, adds an in-depth explanation under the note, with sources.
  - Examples
    > E, or Examples, adds two or three examples under the note, with sources.
  - Map out a node
    > G grows the current node's whole subtree at once and merges it into its branches.
  - Sources
    > The line under a note counts its links and marks dead ones in red. A note with no links says it came from the model's own knowledge.
  - Discuss
    > The box at the bottom of the topic panel talks about the current node. Add to note keeps a reply.
- Look and feel
  > Settings (Ctrl+,) holds the look of the app.
  - Themes
    > Pick a theme in Settings, Appearance. On Omarchy, Mind-Me follows the Omarchy theme by default. Your own themes go in the themes folder of Mind-Me's config folder.
  - Light and dark
    > The sun or moon button in the footer switches the theme's light and dark versions.
  - Size
    > Ctrl+= and Ctrl+- make everything bigger or smaller, Ctrl+0 resets.
  - Fonts
    > Settings, Appearance picks the text and code fonts from the fonts on your computer.
- Files
  > A map is a plain Markdown file on your disk. Any editor can read it.
  - Open and new
    > Ctrl+O opens maps, Ctrl+N makes a new one, and the + menu lists recent maps.
  - Export
    > The + menu copies a map as Markdown or JSON, or saves it with Export.
- Try it
  > Small exercises. Do them here: this map resets each time you open Help.
  - Keep a suggestion under Try it
    > Make Try it current, press M, and click one of the ghosts.
  - Add your own branch
    > Press Tab, type a name, and press Enter.
  - Move a node
    > Drag this node onto Add your own branch.
  - Learn more about a node
    > Make any node current and press L.
  - Delete and undo
    > Delete a node, then press Ctrl+Z to bring it back.
