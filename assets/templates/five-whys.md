# 5 Whys
> Find the root cause of a problem by asking why, five times.
- Problem
  > What exactly goes wrong, and when?
  - Why 1
    > Why does the problem happen?
    - Why 2
      > Why does that happen?
      - Why 3
        > And why does that happen?
        - Why 4
          > Why, once more?
          - Why 5
            > The root cause: what would stop the chain?
- Fix
  > What change removes the root cause?
